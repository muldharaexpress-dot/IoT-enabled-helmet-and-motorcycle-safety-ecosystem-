# Industry Partner Brief: Motorcycle Riders Academy and Safety Consortium

**To:** Motorcycle manufacturers and distributors (Honda, Yamaha, Suzuki, TVS, Bajaj and others)  
**Subject:** Invitation to join the Motorcycle Safety Initiative consortium, and proposed roles  
**Context:** Letter of support from the Road Transport and Highways Division, Memo No. [……] · **Draft:** 18 November 2026

> The roles in this document are **proposals only**. They do not imply that any company has agreed to them. They will be finalised through discussion in a memorandum of understanding (MoU).

---

## 1. Why act now

- **2,609 people were killed in motorcycle crashes in 2024.** That is about 35.8% of all road deaths (Road Safety Foundation). By RSF's counts, deaths rose every year from 2023 to 2025 (2,487 → 2,609 → 2,671).

- **Government policy is tightening.** The 2023 draft Motorcycle Movement Policy proposed a 30 km/h urban speed limit and a licence requirement before purchase (final status still unconfirmed). BRTA issued the Motor Vehicle Speed Limit Guideline in 2024.

- **An opportunity for industry.** If the industry sets up quality training and safer motorcycles itself, it keeps a voice in policy-making and protects its market and brand reputation. Every brand gains when the market is protected from crash and restriction risks.

**The consortium's core idea:** bring separate initiatives under **one common curriculum, one common certificate and one assessment standard**. Each brand can keep its own centres and branding.

## 3. Three-level training (common curriculum)

| Level | Who | Duration (proposed) | Core content |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | New riders, learners | 20 h (6 theory + 10 range + 4 road) | Control, braking with CBS/ABS, emergency stop, helmet and gear, risk and attitude |
| **Secondary** | Licensed riders, ride-hailing/delivery, corporate fleets | 16 h (4 + 6 + 6) | Hazard perception, city and highway riding, advanced braking, fatigue, post-crash response |
| **Advanced** | Advanced riders, **instructors**, specialists | Instructor certification ≥ 60 h | Teaching methods, assessment, range management; renewal every 2 years |

Basis: the WHO manual on powered two- and three-wheeler safety (2022), the graduated licensing model (EU Directive 2006/126/EC), and the Goals for Driver Education (GDE) matrix.

## 4. Proposed contributions from members

| Area | Proposed contribution |
|---|---|
| **Training centres** | Each member provides at least one centre or range in a pilot district (own site or through its dealer network) |
| **Instructors** | Nominate instructor candidates; convert existing instructors to the common certificate (Honda's experience can help) |
| **Training motorcycles** | ≤125 cc motorcycles with CBS/ABS (Primary level) and higher-capacity motorcycles (Advanced level) |
| **Funding** | CSR contributions; a fixed amount per motorcycle sold (subject to government policy approval); subsidised fees for women and low-income riders |
| **Point of sale** | **Two BSTI-standard helmets** with every motorcycle (rider and pillion; as in India's June 2025 draft proposal), a Primary-level training voucher, a safety booklet |
| **Vehicle technology** | **A phased ABS roadmap**: first ABS above 125 cc and ABS/CBS at or below, then ABS on all models (in India the first step took effect on 1 April 2018). Take part in setting the technical standard for IoT-helmet connectivity |
| **Riding simulators** | Simulators at dealerships and schools for theory and hazard-perception training (modelled on Astra Honda's 940 simulators in Indonesia) |

## 5. "One-touch safe motorcycle for all" — joint preparation

- **Goal:** At the moment of purchase, the buyer gets a safer motorcycle (CBS/ABS), a certified or IoT helmet, protective gear and training, all together.

- **A common connectivity standard:** a **brand-neutral, open technical standard** for connecting helmets and motorcycles, so that any brand's helmet works with any motorcycle. The consortium's technical sub-committee will develop it.

- **Helmet interlock:** the motorcycle will not start unless a helmet is worn. It will be piloted first on training motorcycles only, and extended only after its reliability and safety are verified.

- **Data:** anonymised data used for research only with the rider's written consent. No commercial data shared between competing brands.

## 6. Consortium governance

- **Board:** one representative from each member company, plus BRTA, Police (Traffic), BUET-ARI and a road-safety NGO.

- **Sub-committees:** curriculum and assessment; technology and standards; finance and audit.

- **Competition law:** governance will comply with competition law. No discussion of prices, market share or sales plans.

- **Independent evaluation:** results will be evaluated and published by an independent research institution (proposed: BUET-ARI).
