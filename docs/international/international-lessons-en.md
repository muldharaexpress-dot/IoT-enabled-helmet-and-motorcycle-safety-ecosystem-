# International Experience: What Bangladesh Can Learn

**Motorcycle rider training and safety: 8 countries and one evidence review**
Draft · 18 November 2026 · Online check

> **How to read this:** every fact has a source. Check anything marked "unconfirmed" against the original government source before citing it. No reliable information was found on Japan, so it is left out.

---

## 1. At a glance: what other countries do

| Country | Core measure | Verified facts |
|---|---|---|
| **United Kingdom** | Compulsory Basic Training (CBT) before riding as a learner | Compulsory since 1 December 1990. Five elements (A–E); the last is **at least 2 hours on the road** with an instructor. Usually 6–8 hours. Certificate (DL196) **valid for 2 years** |
| **Australia (Victoria)** | Graduated licensing system (GLS) | **2-day compulsory course** before the learner permit (off-road training and an on-road assessment). Learners ride only approved lower-power motorcycles (LAMS). Headlight on at all times. Further conditions in secondary sources: zero alcohol, no pillion, L-plates |
| **Malaysia** | Training at an accredited driving institute, then a government test | Per the government portal, **6 hours circuit + 10 hours road** training. Tests are run by JPJ examiners. After passing, a **2-year probationary licence (PDL)** |
| **Philippines** | Compulsory theory and practical courses | **15 hours theory + at least 8 hours practical** for all applicants. The government set **fee caps** (motorcycle practical course ₱2,500, theory ₱1,000; April 2023). **Biometric attendance** before and after every session. Theory course free at government offices |
| **Vietnam** | Helmets compulsory on all roads (2007) | Adult helmet wearing in four major cities rose from about **30% to over 90%**. A Cu Chi district study found a death rate ratio of **0.69** (95% CI 0.53–0.89). **680,000** fines for not wearing a helmet in 2008. No national surveys were carried out, however |
| **India** | Mandatory braking technology and helmets on vehicles | March 2016 notification: from **1 April 2018**, ABS above 125 cc, and ABS or CBS at or below 125 cc. A June 2025 **draft** proposes two things: ABS on **all** two-wheelers from January 2026, and **two BIS-standard helmets** at purchase (rider and pillion). *Final Gazette status unconfirmed* |
| **Indonesia** | Manufacturer-run safety centres (Astra Honda) | About **9 Safety Riding Centres**, about **120 instructors**, **940 riding simulators**. A claim of 23.4 million people trained comes from one source only, *unconfirmed* |
| **Thailand** | A warning example | About **70–74%** of road deaths are riders of two- and three-wheelers. One estimate puts unlicensed riders at about **10 million**. Critics point to short training, no road test and weak enforcement |

**Research evidence (Cochrane review, 2010):** after analysing 23 studies, the authors could not establish whether training, or which type of training, reduces crashes. Training is still needed to teach basic control. Mandatory pre-licence training may also reduce risk indirectly, because fewer high-risk riders get onto the road.

## 2. What is new for Bangladesh: additions to our framework

Our current framework already has three-level training, an academy, the IoT helmet and a pilot. Below is **what our documents do not yet have**, or cover only weakly:

| # | New lesson | From | Proposal for Bangladesh |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Learner conditions** | Australia (Victoria) | While a learner: no pillion, zero alcohol, L-plate, headlight on. Only ≤125 cc motorcycles with CBS/ABS |
| 2 | **Certificate validity** | United Kingdom | Primary-level certificate valid for 2 years; without a full licence by then, the course must be repeated |
| 3 | **Probationary licence** | Malaysia | First 2 years after licensing are probationary, with stricter action for serious offences |
| 4 | **Fee cap** | Philippines | Government sets a maximum training fee so low-income riders are not excluded. Theory course free at government offices |
| 5 | **Biometric attendance** | Philippines | Fingerprint or face check-in at every session; effective against **fake certificates** |
| 6 | **Two helmets at purchase** | India (draft) | Two BSTI-standard helmets with every motorcycle, for rider and pillion. Fits directly with our "one-touch safe motorcycle" pillar |
| 7 | **Phased ABS** | India | First ABS on higher-capacity and ABS/CBS on smaller motorcycles, then ABS on all. A time-bound roadmap for Bangladesh |
| 8 | **Simulator training** | Indonesia | Riding simulators for theory and hazard perception. They reach more people at low cost and can sit in schools and dealerships |
| 9 | **Law + enforcement + quality together** | Vietnam | Not the law alone: broad enforcement, helmet quality control, and before/after observation surveys |
| 10 | **Mandatory road test** | Lesson from Thailand's weak points | Not only a written test; BRTA's final test covers both range and road |
| 11 | **Rigorous evaluation design** | Cochrane 2010 | A control group in the pilot (already in our framework); a randomised district-level design where feasible |

## 3. What our framework already has (matches other countries)

- **Training time:** 20 hours proposed for the Primary level, close to Malaysia's 16 practical hours and the Philippines' 23 hours (15 + 8).

- **Road training:** 4 hours on the road in our proposal; the UK minimum is 2 hours.

- **Training before licensing:** our proposal (a certificate for the learner licence) matches the UK and Victoria models.

- **Industry-run centres:** our consortium model resembles Indonesia's Astra Honda model, but ours is **multi-brand with a common certificate**.

**Bangladesh's own innovation:** in none of the countries reviewed is **using IoT-helmet data to measure training quality and to coach riders after the course** part of a national programme. This is a distinctive feature of our proposal and could be valuable for international research too. *(Based on a limited search; we cannot claim that no such initiative exists anywhere.)*

## 4. Unconfirmed or not found

- **India's June 2025 draft:** whether the rules on ABS for all motorcycles and two helmets were finalised, and whether the deadline was extended.

- **Victoria:** the full list of learner conditions could not be matched against the official page.

- **Thailand:** which training-reform proposals took effect is unclear.

- **Indonesia:** the figure of 23.4 million trained comes from a single source.

- **Japan:** no reliable information found on motorcycle training hours or Honda's traffic education centre.

- **Vietnam:** the evidence of fewer deaths is local (Cu Chi district), not national.
